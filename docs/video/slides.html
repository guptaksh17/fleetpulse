FleetPulse

Predicts which brake, powertrain or battery will need service in the next 7 days, prices the risk in dollars, and tells the fleet manager what to fix first.

Connected Vehicle Intelligence Hackathon · Motorq[Team name]
The problem

Fleets find out about failures
when the truck stops.

Unplanned

A roadside breakdown means a tow, an emergency repair and a lost delivery day.

Calendar-based

Scheduled service sends healthy vehicles to the workshop and misses failing ones.

Limited slots

Workshops have fixed capacity. The question is not "what is wrong" but "what should we fix first".
